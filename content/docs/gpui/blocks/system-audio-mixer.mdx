## Installation

```cargo
cargo add audiocn
```

The block is part of the crate: `audiocn::SystemAudioMixer`. To feed it real microphones and system audio, add the `device` feature:

```cargo
cargo add audiocn --features device
```

## What it does

- **Microphone**: choose a device, start and stop it, and see when it is live, muted or blocked.
- **System audio**: a switch that starts and stops capture, with notices when nothing was shared or the platform cannot capture. With the `device` feature, `audiocn::io::use_system_audio` captures the default output.
- **Music**: play and skip tracks. On the demo meters, music ducks by 12 dB while the microphone is live. With your own audio, apply the duck in your graph.
- **Sounds**: a popover of sound pads with hotkeys.
- **Every channel** has a stereo meter, a fader, mute, solo and a monitor send to the speakers. Microphone monitoring is off by default to avoid feedback.
- **Master**: a stereo meter with a clip counter, a master fader and mute. There is no `MediaStream` in GPUI: `on_output_change` tells you which inputs are open, as a `MixerActivity`, so you can attach them to your own graph.
- **Layouts**: switch between rows and console strips.

The block owns no audio graph. Pass a meter per channel, and keep the mixer state in a `MixerStore` to apply its gains to your inputs. Leave anything out and that part runs on its own: demo signals fill the meters, and tracks and pads play on simulated players.

## Usage

```rust
use audiocn::io::{MicrophoneOptions, use_audio_devices, use_microphone};
use audiocn::signal::mixer::{MixerOptions, use_mixer};
use audiocn::{
    AudioDeviceKind, MixerSound, MixerSourceId, MixerTrack, SystemAudioMixer,
    system_audio_mixer_channels,
};

let mixer = use_mixer(
    "studio/mixer",
    MixerOptions { channels: system_audio_mixer_channels(), ..Default::default() },
    window,
    cx,
);
let devices = use_audio_devices("studio/devices", AudioDeviceKind::AudioInput, window, cx);
let mic = use_microphone("studio/mic", MicrophoneOptions::default(), window, cx);
let (start, stop) = (mic.clone(), mic.clone());

SystemAudioMixer::new("studio")
    .mixer(mixer)
    .devices(devices.devices.clone())
    .permission(devices.permission)
    .microphone_status(mic.status.into())
    .on_microphone_start(move |_, cx| start.start(cx))
    .on_microphone_stop(move |_, cx| stop.stop(cx))
    .meter(MixerSourceId::Microphone, mic.meter.clone())
    .on_output_change(|activity, _, _| graph.attach(activity))
    .sounds([MixerSound::new("ding", "Ding").src("/sounds/ding.mp3").hotkey("1")])
    .tracks([MixerTrack::new("intro", "Intro").src("/music/intro.mp3")])
```

Read the strip settings from `mixer.read(cx)` and apply them to each input, for example `handle.set_gain_db(..)` and `handle.set_muted(!store.is_audible("microphone"))` on the microphone. Drive the music and the pads yourself with `music_playing`, `on_music_toggle`, `track_index`, `on_next_track`, `sound_playback` and `on_sound_trigger`, or leave them out to use the built-in simulated players.

## Files

The block lives in `src/blocks/system_audio_mixer.rs` of the `audiocn` crate, with its two strips as public parts you can reuse on their own:

- `MixerSourceStrip`: one source with icon, status, meter, fader, mute, solo and monitor (`.title(..)`, `.mixer(..)`, `.meter(..)`, `.status(..)`, `.actions(..)`, `.notice(..)`, `.monitorable(..)`).
- `MixerMasterStrip`: the master meter, clip count, fader and mute (`.mixer(..)`, `.meter(..)`).

To change more than the builder options allow, copy the file into your app, rename the `crate::` paths to `audiocn::`, and edit it. It uses only the crate's public API.
